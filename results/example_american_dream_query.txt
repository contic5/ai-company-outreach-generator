Dear Hiring Team, 

I’m writing on behalf of Spectrum Works to introduce a partnership opportunity that will help American Dream Mall attract dependable, motivated employees while advancing your goals for outstanding guest service and inclusive hiring. Spectrum Works is a job training and employment program that prepares autistic young adults for competitive employment through a combination of hands-on on-the-job training, classroom-based skills development, and ongoing workplace supports. Our model is employer-centered: we match candidates to roles based on workplace needs, prepare them in real-world environments, and provide job coaching and accommodations so employers can get productive team members with minimal disruption. 

Why Spectrum Works is a strong fit for American Dream Mall 
- Roles we place: retail associates, guest services/concierge, inventory/stock, custodial and facilities support, food-and-beverage team members, and other customer-facing and back-of-house positions. 
- Prepared, dependable employees: Candidates complete workplace-focused training and supervised placements so they arrive ready to contribute. 
- Employer support: We provide individualized job coaching during onboarding and as needed, training on reasonable accommodations, and ongoing communication with supervisors. - Business benefits: Inclusive hiring can improve retention, reduce recruitment costs, strengthen customer service, and positively reflect on corporate social responsibility and community engagement. 
- Flexible approach: We tailor placements to your schedules and operational needs and can arrange trial or internship periods to ensure a strong fit. 

We would welcome the opportunity to discuss specific staffing needs at American Dream Mall and to introduce one or more qualified candidates for a trial placement. Next steps I suggest: 
1) A brief 30-minute call or in-person meeting with your HR or operations manager to review roles, schedules, and any site requirements. 
2) We provide candidate profiles and recommended accommodations for roles of interest. 
3) Arrange a short, supported trial shift so your team can meet candidates on-site.
